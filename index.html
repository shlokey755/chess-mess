Micro-Strategy

1v1 Tactical Grid Battle

The Core

Shrinking grid battle. Last player standing wins. Board loses 1 row/column every 3 turns.

Your Units

♞
Knight — Jumps 2 squares (L-shaped). Hits hard. Your speed pick.
✦
Mage — 2-square range (plus cross). Weak but ranged. Control the midfield.
⚔
Brawler — 1 square all directions. Tough tank (4 HP). Slow but strong.

How to Play

  1. Click a unit to see its movement range (highlighted in yellow)
  2. Click a highlighted square to move or attack
  3. Each player gets 1 move per turn
  4. Attack deals 1 damage (2 for Brawler). Units have 2-4 HP
  5. Every 3 turns the board shrinks — units outside die

Win Condition

Eliminate all enemy units. Or outlast them as the board shrinks.

Pass the device between turns. Player 1 (Blue) goes first.
