Micro-Strategy Chess

Classic Chess with a Shrinking Board

The Core

Standard chess rules apply. Win by checkmate or elimination. Board shrinks from edges every 8 turns, eliminating units caught outside.

Chess Pieces

P
Pawn — Forward 1 (or 2 on first move). Captures diagonally.
R
Rook — Any distance horizontal/vertical.
N
Knight — L-shape (2+1). Can jump pieces.
B
Bishop — Any distance diagonal.
Q
Queen — Rook + Bishop combined.
K
King — 1 square any direction. Win if this dies.

How to Play

  1. Click a piece to highlight its valid moves (yellow squares)
  2. Click a highlighted square to move
  3. Capture enemy pieces by moving to their square
  4. Every 8 turns, board shrinks 1 square from all edges
  5. Units outside the new boundary are eliminated
  6. Win by eliminating the opponent's King

Special Rules

Friendly Fire: You cannot move to squares occupied by your own pieces.

Shrinking Board: At 8-turn intervals, board shrinks from edges. Plan your positioning!

Pass the device between turns. White (Player 1) goes first.
